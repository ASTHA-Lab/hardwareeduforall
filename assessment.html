Deployment record

What 25 students reported, and what that does not prove.

Three modules have enough offerings to talk about student response. The cohorts were not randomized, no section ran without the modules, and the knowledge ratings are self-reported. Counts this small support a deployment report. They do not support a statistical claim.

The instrument changed between the 2024 offerings, so the cohorts are not directly comparable. A response rate is known only for Spring 2024. Spring 2024 respondents were 63% male and 38% female, and 75% identified as Hispanic or Latino. Fall 2024 respondents were all male.

The split on fundamentals is the result to keep. Spring 2024 agreed that the modules helped them understand embedded systems. Fall 2024 mostly did not. Half of the Fall respondents reported not attempting the assignments. The number is small, and it is not a reason to average the two cohorts into one success story.

Four lessons for the next offering

1

The repair is where the comments get specific.

One Spring 2023 student called the overflow assignment among the most interesting and rewarding work in the program, and described working out where execution had to go so that further secrets would return. M1, which has no defense, drew requests for context: a good introduction that did not say what the technique is for.

2

A desktop mitigation does not survive the move to an 8051.

The lecture names stack canaries and address-space layout randomization. Neither exists on this target. The repair the student writes has to hold on the bare machine. A mitigation is a property of a platform.

3

Scaffolding binds harder than equipment.

The 2024 overflow handout adds a partial-credit ladder and procedural hints. Both are cheaper than a second course, and both answer the barrier the surveys actually recorded.

4

The toolchain has to run on the machines students have.

Free responses include a student who could not run the vendor tools on a personal machine. The three deployed embedded modules share one 8-bit target and one vendor environment. Whether the same ideas carry to a 32-bit core is untested.
