Host courses

The modules are a component of a course, not a course of their own.

Each host course keeps its syllabus, exams, and grading. The security work replaces an assignment the course would have given anyway, in the week that assignment belongs.

EECS 388 · University of Kansas

Embedded systems

Target is the Microchip AT89C51, programmed and debugged in Keil. Spring 2024 ran M1, M2, and M3 as required work, two points each. Spring 2023 ran M1 as homework and M2 as a bonus. M4 is specified for the same course and has not been assigned.

  1. M1 Binary reverse engineering
  2. M2 Buffer overflow
  3. M3 Timing side channel
  4. M4 Board reverse engineering

Digital logic design

From gates to a UART

M5 has an earlier life as a simulator homework. M6, M7, and M8 are VHDL laboratories adapted from hardware-security lab material and from a published tutorial on obfuscation and logic locking. They introduce a register-transfer flow the microcontroller modules never reach. They have not yet been surveyed.

  1. M5 Power side channel
  2. M6 Combinational locking
  3. M7 Hardware Trojan
  4. M8 Sequential locking

Computer architecture · Fall 2024

Cache timing

M9 ran as two lecture parts with a recorded measurement on a commodity x86 machine. No survey was given, and the source notes do not state a graded assignment.

  1. M9 Cache side channel

How the suite grew

Spring 2022

Side-channel content as a paper and simulator exercise. An early form of the power module.

Spring 2023

Binary reverse engineering required. Buffer overflow offered as a bonus. Internal survey, 13 responses.

Spring 2024

M1, M2, and M3 each have a two-part lecture and a required assignment. External evaluation, 8 of 13 students.

Fall 2024

The same three embedded modules again, plus the cache lecture in computer architecture. External evaluation, 4 responses.

Prepared

Board reverse engineering, both locking labs, and the Trojan lab. Written, not yet surveyed.

What changed in the grade book

In Spring 2023 the reverse-engineering homework carried 10.5 points and the overflow bonus carried 20. In Spring 2024 each of the three assignments carried 2 points. One student skipped the bonus and was surprised to meet the material on the final. A module chosen because it exercises a core concept is examinable by construction. Leaving it optional sends two signals at once.

The cap is three modules in a semester, so the security work does not become a second course hiding inside the first.
